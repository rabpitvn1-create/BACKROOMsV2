## 1. Identity and authority

- SRU means Special Response Unit / Lực lượng Phản ứng Đặc biệt.
- SRU was founded in 2099, 200 years before the 2299 main-era events.
- Founder: Eric Ko.
- Headquarters: Vatican, Italy.
- Organizational chain: Hội Đồng Bảo An Liên Không Thời Gian -> Cảnh Sát chống hiện tượng dị thường -> SRU.
- Hội Đồng Bảo An Liên Không Thời Gian directly assigns missions to SRU.
- Black Vatican is not SRU's command authority. It provides training, military/manpower support and deployment support.
- Huyết Nha is another force available to the Council, but it generally cannot handle the highest-tier missions normally assigned to SRU.

## 2. Number Ø and the Thirteen Numbers

- Number Ø is Sparda. Number Ø is above the Thirteen Numbers and is not counted among I-XIII.
- Sparda is a primordial ruler of Hell with power close to the Creator. The thirteen Princes of Hell rule their own infernal sub-worlds under him.
- The combat core of SRU consists of thirteen Numbers I-XIII.
- Each Number position represents one of thirteen Princes of Hell, primarily by power/ability correspondence.
- Number labels are not a power ranking. All thirteen Numbers occupy roughly the same combat tier, with only small differences.
- Any Number can directly fight at a scale comparable to a planetary catastrophe.
- A fight between two Numbers is not expected to be one-sided. Even a stronger Number may have to pay an extreme price to defeat another.

## 3. Number XIII, Vassago and Liz

- Number XIII originally belongs to the Vassago representation.
- Liz originally held Number XIII as Vassago's representative.
- Kai likes the number thirteen. During a drinking session with Vassago, Kai asked to take XIII for himself.

## 4. Command structure

- Kai Akechi and Eric Ko both possess command authority over SRU.
- Kai is the real top commander in substance, but he habitually pushes administration and daily reports onto Eric. Outsiders can therefore mistake Eric for the sole commander.
- Ordinary SRU orders require authorization through both Kai and Eric.
- If one of them loses the ability to command, the other gains decision authority.
- If Kai and Eric reach an irreconcilable command conflict, they may settle it by direct combat. The one still standing makes the final decision. Lethal force between them is allowed if necessary.
- Once Kai and Eric jointly issue the final order, a Number cannot refuse the objective.
- The assigned Number retains broad sovereignty over method, tactics, force level and execution details unless the final order explicitly constrains them.
- The Thirteen Numbers are not a perfectly coordinated team. Each has an independent strategic layer and may strongly disagree before a final order exists.

Approved doctrine label:

OBJECTIVE ABSOLUTE / METHOD SOVEREIGN
- The final objective is mandatory once jointly authorized.
- Execution method remains primarily the assigned Number's decision.

Approved command label:

RIGHT OF THE LAST STANDING
- In an irreconcilable Kai-Eric command dispute, the last one able to remain standing and continue decides.

## 5. Mission acceptance

- Hội Đồng Bảo An Liên Không Thời Gian directly offers/assigns missions to SRU.
- Kai and Eric may reject a mission or alter its objective, scope or conditions before joint approval.
- Their right to reject remains valid in every case, including absolute emergencies.
- If both reject a mission, the Council may transfer it to another force.
- In practice, very few other forces are capable of accepting the highest-tier missions intended for SRU.

## 6. Consequences and Last Judgement

- SRU does not use one universal collateral-damage threshold. Consequences are judged in the context of each mission.
- Serious violations include deliberate massacre or intentionally allowing a target to cause severe harm when the Number could reasonably intervene.
- A Number accused of such conduct may be summoned to Last Judgement.
- Last Judgement is judged by Sparda and the thirteen Princes of Hell.
- Formal judgment uses majority voting.
- In practice, Sparda's overwhelming primordial power and status make his position close to the final word. Princes may disagree internally but rarely risk open defiance because Sparda could erase their infernal sub-worlds if truly enraged.
- Punishment can range from a written reprimand to permanent exile in Hell.
- Numbers are generally not foolish enough to massacre or destroy a planet merely for personal amusement.

Approved review concept:

AFTERMATH ASSESSMENT
- Review whether the objective was completed.
- Separate necessary consequences from avoidable consequences.
- Consider what alternatives were realistically available to the Number at the time.
- Consider whether the Number acted for the mission or for personal motives.
- Only sufficiently serious cases escalate to Last Judgement.

Approved Last Judgement flow:

1. ACCUSATION - present the event, final order, evidence and realistic options.
2. TESTIMONY - the accused Number may explain the decision without being forced to apologize.
3. JUDGEMENT - Sparda and the thirteen Princes vote.

## 7. SRU deployment doctrine

- SRU normally avoids deploying more Numbers than necessary.
- A single Number can be sufficient for many SRU-class missions.
- Multiple Numbers are used when the problem requires distinct simultaneous capabilities, broad coverage or a threat beyond one Number's practical handling.
- Deploying several planetary-catastrophe-tier combatants can itself increase operational risk.
- Before the future event "The BACKROOMS", all thirteen Numbers have never fought in the same operation at once.

Approved full-deployment protocol:

THIRTEENFOLD
- Reserved for full Thirteen Numbers deployment.
- All Numbers leave lower-priority missions.
- Black Vatican opens full support capacity.
- Liz switches SRU logistics to emergency deployment mode.
- Number Ø / Sparda is immediately notified.
- The future event "The BACKROOMS" is the first time all thirteen Numbers must participate in combat together.

## 8. SRU base and Black Vatican training

Approved SRU expansion:

- SRU headquarters is in Vatican, Italy, with protected operational areas not represented on ordinary civilian maps.
- Command Chamber is the central command/authorization area used heavily by Eric for daily operation and final-order processing.
- Number Hall is a hardened area for the Numbers, with spaces adapted to their abnormal power characteristics.
- Zero Gate is a restricted link associated with Number Ø, Hell and Last Judgement. It is not routine transport.

Black Vatican training priorities include:

- power-output control;
- limiting unnecessary environmental destruction;
- combat near civilians and protected objectives;
- fighting alongside another Number without uncontrolled interference;
- solving low-power targets without using catastrophic force;
- operating under mission-specific restrictions.

The training problem is often not "how to hit harder" but "how to destroy only what must be destroyed".

## 9. Internal culture

- The Thirteen Numbers are not automatically friends, family or a perfectly synchronized squad.
- They may reject proposals, criticize plans and argue strongly before a final order is jointly authorized.
- Once Kai and Eric jointly authorize the final order, debate about whether to execute the objective ends.
- Numbers are expected to retain their own tactical judgment during execution.
- Respect inside the group often comes from capability and demonstrated judgment rather than Number rank.

## 10. Liz logistics

- Liz is an official SRU member and the confirmed logistics exception outside the Thirteen Numbers.
- She is the SRU-side logistics authority/coordination point, while Black Vatican and other supporting bodies can provide personnel, equipment and transport as needed.
- "XIII 1/2" remains Kai's unofficial nickname for her.
- Deeper identity, ability and history fields remain OPEN.
