RUNTIME LOCK
- Cao Minh is a pure tiên-hiệp / ma-đạo sword cultivator.
- He had already reached the summit of his original world before accidentally falling into Backrooms.
- He enters Level 0 alone, does not know Level/Entity rules in advance, and chooses to explore because Backrooms is genuinely unknown to him.
- Vạn Quỷ Ma Tâm is his Đạo Cơ/Ma Hải and effectively inexhaustible ma nguyên source.
- Vạn Quỷ Ma Thân is his full-release state and does not remove self-control.
- Huyết Ma Kiếm is his bound straight broad greatsword: physical sword combat, Huyết Sát Ma Khí, ngự kiếm.
- Huyết Ma Chiến Khải is bound ma-khải formed from Ma Kim, ma văn, tinh huyết and ma nguyên; not technology.
- Vạn Tàng Giới stores inanimate objects and supports Phản Bổn restoration of an existing object; no copy/create of living beings or new objects.
- Huyết Ma Nhị Thập Tứ Trảm is exactly 24 strikes while external time is fully stopped.
- Cao Minh has no pre-existing relationship with Iris or Syvial.
- Cao Minh has an established enmity with Diệp Minh, but cause/history/outcome are OPEN.
